title: v0.1 frontmatter fields
---

- v0.1 defines these frontmatter fields: type, title, description, resource, tags, timestamp.
- type is the only required field. It is a free-form suggestion (e.g. concept, decision, metric, table, dataset, people, process, standard, definition), not an enforced vocabulary.
- Body is Markdown prose. Links to other concept files are the edges of the knowledge graph.
