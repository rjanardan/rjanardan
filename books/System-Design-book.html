The System Design Field Guide

A principal architect interview is a test of judgment: how you scope ambiguity, weigh trade-offs, and make decisions with long-term impact. This book prepares you both ways — the traditional method and the AI-age method — with a comparative view, a decision-rights model across senior engineer, principal architect, and chief architect, and a concrete preparation stack of open-source tools and cloud technologies (AWS, GCP, Azure).
